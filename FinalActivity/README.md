# Final Activity: Operations Runbook and Maintenance Checklist

This 60-minute capstone consolidates evidence from Modules 1–4 into a runbook another operator can execute without workshop context.

## Deliverables

1. Copy [Runbook-Template.md](Runbook-Template.md) to a working document.
2. Complete every required section using tested commands and observed evidence.
3. Complete [Exercises.md](Exercises.md), including the integrated incident.
4. Review [Exercises-Solutions.md](Exercises-Solutions.md) only after submission.

## Quality Standard

Every check or alert must state:

- scope and purpose;
- exact signal or command;
- normal condition and actionable threshold;
- owner and escalation path;
- first response;
- recovery and verification;
- destructive-risk or single-node warning where relevant.

A command list without decisions, owners, thresholds, and verification is not a runbook.

## Suggested Timing

- 10 minutes: choose service objectives and ownership.
- 20 minutes: complete daily checks and alert matrix.
- 20 minutes: write incident and maintenance procedures.
- 10 minutes: peer review using the completion checklist.
